# DerivHack Hackathon History

**Classification:** Historical Documentation
**Events:** DerivHack 2018, DerivHack 2019

---

## Overview

DerivHack was a series of hackathons organized to explore practical implementation of the ISDA Common Domain Model (CDM) on distributed ledger technology (DLT) platforms.

---

## DerivHack 2018

### Event Details

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Date** | September 20-21, 2018 |
| **Locations** | London and New York (simultaneous) |
| **Participants** | 31 teams, 140 coders |
| **Organizers** | Barclays, Deloitte, ISDA, Thomson Reuters |

### Purpose

To get feedback on the emerging CDM standard, explore practical implementation of ideas, and assess the feasibility of applying the CDM to emerging technologies.

### Use Cases

Participants were provided several use cases:
1. Set-up counterparty data
2. New trade event
3. Trade events
4. Lifecycle events
5. Settlement
6. Reporting

### Winners - London

| Award | Winner | Solution |
|-------|--------|----------|
| **Overall Winner** | Digital Asset | DAML-based solution solving all six use cases |
| **Best Pitch** | INDUSTRIA | Design for Smarter Derivatives Market |
| **Best Solution Architecture** | blk.io | Built on Epirus platform |

### Winners - New York

| Award | Winner |
|-------|--------|
| **Overall Winner** | Baton Systems |
| **Best Solution Architecture** | Chatham Financial |

### Key Technologies Used

| Platform | Teams Using |
|----------|-------------|
| DAML (Digital Asset) | Multiple including winner |
| Corda (R3) | Multiple teams |
| Ethereum | Some teams |
| Other DLT | Various |

## DerivHack 2019

### Event Details

| Attribute | Detail |
|-----------|--------|
| **Organizers** | R3, Barclays, ISDA |
| **Locations** | Singapore, London (multiple locations) |
| **Preparation** | Two months of development |
| **Event Duration** | Two days |

### Focus

Building on 2018 success, with emphasis on:
- CDM on Corda platform
- Expanded geographic reach
- More mature CDM implementations

### Winners

INDUSTRIA's implementation of Corda and CDM platform helped teams win in 2 out of 3 locations:
- **Singapore** - Winner
- **London** - Winner

## Industry Impact

### Estimated Savings

Potential efficiencies from technologies demonstrated at DerivHack could save the sector approximately **US $3 billion per year**.

### CDM Savings Estimate (with Blockchain)

During the 2018-2019 period, it was estimated that the CDM could save the industry **$2.5 billion** if combined with blockchain.

## Legacy and Impact

### CDM Development

DerivHack events contributed to:
- Validation of CDM practical implementation
- Identification of technology requirements
- Community building around CDM
- Proof-of-concept for regulatory reporting

### Industry Adoption

Organizations that participated in DerivHack went on to contribute to:
- FINOS CDM open source project
- ISDA DRR development
- Production implementations
