Standardization
The ISDA Clause Library will introduce greater standardization in the way firms negotiate and agree terms in their contracts and how they 
categorize and manage legal agreement data.
Benefits include:
• Quicker and more efficient contract negotiation and client on-boarding;
• More accurate and efficient regulatory and market-driven repapering exercises;
• More consistent and accurate reporting of legal agreement data – for example, under qualified financial contract record-keeping 
requirements or as part of supervisory or prudential oversight rules.
The ISDA Clause Library provides standard-form drafting options for commonly negotiated provisions within 
ISDA documents and the most common variants of those provisions. Increased standardization is a key 
component of ISDA’s strategy for developing enhanced legal documentation standards, delivering digitized 
documents through ISDA Create and facilitating further automation of derivatives products through the 
development of smart derivatives contracts.

Digitization
Greater standardization creates opportunities to optimize and automate business processes through the digitization of documentation.
Benefits include:
• Creation of an industry standard framework for categorizing 
and managing legal agreement data;
• Integration with the ISDA Common Domain Model (CDM). This 
will provide greater alignment with systems and processes 
designed to monitor important business and operational 
functions deriving from legal contracts, including netting and 
collateral enforceability, liquidity and counterparty credit risk;
• Digitization of legal documentation and the development 
and application of new technologies such as distributed 
ledger, smart contracts and artificial intelligence to derivatives 
documentation and products.
Distribution:
• The ISDA Clause Library will be updated and maintained to reflect evolving market, legal and documentation practices.
• The library is available in the ISDA bookstore and has also been integrated with the ISDA Create platform.
