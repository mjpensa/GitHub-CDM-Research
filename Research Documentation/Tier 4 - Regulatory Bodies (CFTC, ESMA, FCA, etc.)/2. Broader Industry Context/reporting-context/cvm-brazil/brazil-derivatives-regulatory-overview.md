# Brazil Derivatives Regulatory Overview

**Date:** December 2025
**Jurisdiction:** Brazil
**Primary Regulator:** CVM (Comissão de Valores Mobiliários - Brazilian Securities and Exchange Commission)

---

## Regulatory Framework

### Trade Reporting Requirements

All transactions must be reported to a registered trade repository. The obligations apply to:
- All OTC derivatives entered into by Brazilian entities
- Cross-border transactions where one counterparty is a Brazilian entity

### Legal Validity Requirement

**Unique Brazilian Requirement:**
Law 6,385/76 provides that in order for derivative contracts admitted for trading in Brazil to be legally valid, they must be registered with registration entities authorized by the Brazilian Central Bank or the CVM.

**Important Note:** This validity condition is not commonly found in other countries, so foreign institutions may not be accustomed to it.

---

## Clearing Requirements

Brazil is one of six jurisdictions (out of 44 surveyed) that have mandatory clearing requirements for OTC derivatives.

**Other jurisdictions with mandatory clearing:**
- Bulgaria
- Hungary
- India
- Mexico
- Poland

---

## Initial Margin Requirements (2024)

### Threshold Calculation

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Threshold | BRL 25 billion |
| Calculation Period | March, April, May 2024 |
| Calculation Method | Daily (not month-end) |

Initial margin requirements apply to:
- Financial institutions authorized to operate by the Central Bank of Brazil
- Entities with average (daily) aggregate notional amount exceeding the threshold

**Note:** Brazil uses a daily calculation (rather than month-end) for the average aggregate notional amount.

## Market Infrastructure

### B3 (Brasil, Bolsa, Balcão)

B3 is the sole domestic Central Clearing Counterparty (CCP) in Brazil.

**Functions:**
- Central clearing
- Trade registration
- Trade repository services

### Investment Fund Statistics (April 30, 2024)

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Investment funds with OTC positions outside B3 | 258 |
| Total AUM of these funds | R$1,246 billion |
| Counterparty concentration | 95% of transactions involve 14 large financial institutions |

---

## Investment Fund Regulation

### Resolution 175/2022

CVM Resolution 175/2022 specifies leverage limits for certain types of investment funds.

### Fund Capital Risk (FCR)

CVM and B3 developed the Fund Capital Risk (FCR) metric to:
- Evaluate investment funds' leverage
- Compare against leverage limits set under Resolution 175/2022

## CDM/DRR Relevance

### Current Status

Brazil is not currently listed in ISDA's Digital Regulatory Reporting (DRR) coverage or near-term development roadmap.

### Potential Considerations

Given Brazil's:
- Mandatory clearing requirements
- Unique registration validity requirements
- Growing derivatives market

There may be future interest in standardization efforts, though regulatory alignment with global standards (like ISO 20022 and CDM) would require significant adaptation.

---

## Cross-Border Considerations

### Foreign Institutions

Foreign institutions entering the Brazilian derivatives market should be aware of:

1. **Registration Requirement:** Derivatives must be registered for legal validity
2. **Counterparty Status:** Understanding Brazilian counterparty classification
3. **Clearing Obligations:** Mandatory clearing for certain products
4. **Trade Reporting:** Mandatory reporting to registered trade repository
