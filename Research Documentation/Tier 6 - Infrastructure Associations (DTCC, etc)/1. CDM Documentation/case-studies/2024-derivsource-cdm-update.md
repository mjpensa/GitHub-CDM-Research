## Full Article Content

The Common Domain Model (CDM) represents a standardized framework for representing transaction events across derivatives, securities lending, repo, and bond markets. Since 2017, the International Swaps and Derivatives Association (ISDA) has championed this initiative to create a unified digital representation of trade lifecycle events.

### Purpose and Benefits

CDM adoption aims to reduce reconciliation burdens and minimize data inconsistencies that could lead to settlement failures. By providing a single digital representation, it enhances consistency and interoperability across institutions and platforms, creating a foundation for technological innovation.

The International Securities Lending Association (ISLA) and International Capital Markets Association (ICMA) have extended CDM principles to securities lending, repo, and bond transactions, developing digital versions of industry standard master agreements.

### Post-Trade Applications

Axoni's Ishan Singh notes that CDM serves as "a platform on which firms can enhance the efficiency of the tools they already have." The standardized model enables consistent processing across asset classes, benefiting reporting and reconciliation. Real-time CDM data delivery to banking and asset management systems supports informed decision-making.

ISDA's Tara Kruse indicates the industry has reached a tipping point where growing adoption generates network effects encouraging broader participation.

### Digital Regulatory Reporting (DRR)

CDM underpins ISDA's DRR initiative, adopted as a regulatory reporting standard across multiple jurisdictions. Rather than individual firm interpretations, DRR delivers standardized, machine-executable code enabling automatic compliance with regulations.

**Recent Regulatory Updates:**
- CFTC version 3.2 (January 2024): Unique Product Identifier requirements
- Japan FSA (April 2024): Expanded reporting regime
- EU EMIR Refit (April 2024): Increased reportable fields from 125 to 203

### Collateral Management Integration

Vermeg has integrated CDM into its Colline platform, enabling streamlined initial margin CSA consumption. Data processed through four-eyes validation becomes available within minutes for derivatives margining—significantly reducing onboarding time and costs.

Chris Rayner from ISLA emphasizes that "standardisation is a key factor not only in maintaining efficiency of the securities lending market but also in solidifying its resilience."

### Open Source Governance

In September 2022, FINOS was selected to host CDM resources as open source, providing a neutral space where industry experts contribute equally. This democratization has enabled smaller firms to access and implement CDM frameworks independently.

### Future Focus Areas

ISDA priorities through 2025 include:
- Digital representation of legal documents
- Collateral eligibility standardization
- Regulatory updates for Hong Kong, Canada, MiFIR, and MAS/ASIC regimes
- Document digitization campaigns for ISDA Master Agreements and CSAs

The industry increasingly recognizes that legacy infrastructure cannot support T+1 settlement and enhanced regulatory scrutiny, positioning CDM as critical infrastructure for modernized financial markets.
