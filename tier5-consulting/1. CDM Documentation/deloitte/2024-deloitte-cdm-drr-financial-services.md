# Deloitte: CDM and Digital Regulatory Reporting

**Source:** Deloitte UK Financial Services Insights
**Date:** 2024
**URL:** https://ukfinancialservicesinsights.deloitte.com/

## Overview

Deloitte has been tracking the development and adoption of the ISDA Common Domain Model (CDM) since its early stages. Their UK Financial Services Insights platform provides coverage of CDM developments, including the successful testing of Digital Regulatory Reporting (DRR) with BNP Paribas.

## Key Insights

### CDM Industry Investment

Since Deloitte's initial coverage in 2019, the industry has collectively invested significant time and effort in the development of the ISDA Common Domain Model (CDM), taking it from proof of concept to practical applications:

- **Digital Regulatory Reporting for CFTC Rewrite** - providing a foundation for DRR
- **Common Risk Interchange Format (CRIF)** - supporting benchmarking of the FRTB Standardized Approach (Risk Magazine 2022 award for Innovation in Technology)
- **Cross-asset class expansion** - extending beyond OTC Derivatives by being adopted by ICMA and ISLA

### Industry Adoption Scale

The CDM is an open-source model that has incorporated contributions of technology and design from many organizations and is available in multiple different programming languages as a free download:

| Metric | Value |
|--------|-------|
| Top-tier institutions onboard | 30+ |
| Leading industry experts | 100+ |
| Industry person-hours invested | 32,000+ |

### BNP Paribas DRR Testing

Deloitte reported on the successful testing of Digital Regulatory Reporting using the Common Domain Model between ISDA and BNP Paribas. This milestone demonstrated the practical viability of CDM for regulatory compliance.

## Deloitte's Perspective on CDM Benefits

1. **Standardization** - Common data representation across the industry
2. **Automation** - Machine-executable regulatory reporting
3. **Cost Reduction** - Reduced implementation and compliance costs
4. **Accuracy** - Elimination of interpretation differences
