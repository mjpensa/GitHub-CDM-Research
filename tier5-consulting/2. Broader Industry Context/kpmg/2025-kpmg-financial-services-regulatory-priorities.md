# 2025 Financial Services Regulatory Priorities

## Full Article Content

The article examines the financial services regulatory landscape heading into 2025, noting that both the UK Government and European co-legislators aim to reshape their regulatory frameworks with competitiveness and growth as focal points.

### UK Government's Growth Strategy

The UK Government plans to unveil its Financial Services Growth and Competitiveness Strategy in spring 2025, targeting five priority sectors: fintech, sustainable finance, asset management and wholesale services, insurance and reinsurance, and capital markets including retail investment. This initiative, signaled through the Chancellor's Mansion House speech, reflects governmental intent to stimulate the sector while maintaining financial stability and consumer protection objectives.

### EU Competitiveness Focus

The European Union similarly seeks to enhance competitiveness with its new Financial Services Commissioner working to mobilize financing for green, digital, and social transitions.

### Banking and Financial Resilience

Basel reforms implementation began January 1, 2025, in the EU through the amended Capital Requirements Regulation. The UK deferred its implementation to January 2027, citing ongoing US policy uncertainty. Both jurisdictions emphasize robust risk management, particularly regarding credit risk, liquidity monitoring, and stress testing. The PRA announced plans to reduce bank reporting requirements while maintaining vigilance on financial stability.

### Insurance Sector Developments

UK Solvency II implementation is complete. The PRA now focuses on solvent exit planning requirements and enhanced liquidity reporting for certain insurers. EIOPA and the European Commission continue developing technical standards for the revamped Solvency II Directive and the new Insurance Resolution and Recovery Directive, emphasizing that risk-based capital requirements remain necessary for stability.

### Wealth and Asset Management

UK asset managers will navigate the phased rollout of the FCA's Sustainability Disclosure Requirements, while EU firms address ESMA's fund naming guidance. Implementation of AIFMD II progresses toward the 2026 deadline, with increased scrutiny on valuation practices in private asset management.

### Capital Markets Evolution

The UK's simplified listing regime launched in July 2024. The FCA aims to finalize rules for the Public Offers and Admissions to Trading Regime by mid-2025 to replace the Prospectus Regime. In the EU, the Listing Act package implementation deadline is June 6, 2026. Both jurisdictions are modernizing reporting requirements for trades and transactions, with the UK's bonds and derivatives transparency regime changes applying from December 1, 2025.

### Consumer Duty Intensification

The FCA maintained unprecedented supervisory intensity throughout 2024 assessing Consumer Duty alignment. The regulator identified fundamental gaps in outcomes testing, pricing, complaints handling, and board reporting. Additional reviews on vulnerable customer consideration, customer support, and closed product implementation are forthcoming in 2025, likely driving continued investigation and enforcement activity.

### Operational Resilience Milestones

The EU's Digital Operational Resilience Act (DORA) became fully effective January 17, 2025, significantly impacting financial entities. Critical third-party risk management received heightened attention, with UK designations beginning January 1. Financial Market Infrastructures must comply with Bank of England outsourcing and third-party risk management statements by February 9, while firms must demonstrate they remain within impact tolerances under severe stress scenarios by March 2025.

### Payments Ecosystem

The Authorised Push Payment fraud reimbursement rules are now enforced, with regulators monitoring implementation outcomes. The UK National Payments Vision, published in Q4 2024, provides strategic direction for Open Banking and payments infrastructure modernization. The Payments Vision Delivery Committee will outline system upgrade approaches by Q2 2025. The EU finalizes Payment Services Directive 3 and Payment Services Regulation in early 2025, with likely 2026 implementation for payment service providers.

### Sustainable Finance and ESG

Corporate Sustainability Reporting Directive reporting commenced January 1, 2025, with transition plan disclosure requirements expanding further under future directives. The UK will consult on UK Sustainability Reporting Standards in March with minimal deviations anticipated. Prudential supervisors expect enhanced climate-related risk measurement and management, with the PRA consulting on revised guidance in Q1 2025. EIOPA recommendations regarding fossil fuel sector exposures and biodiversity risk management approach finalization are expected.

### Digital Finance Innovation

The EU's Markets in Cryptoassets Regulation applied fully December 30, 2024, with ongoing technical standard guidance. The UK Government committed to phased cryptoasset regulation, consolidating previous stablecoin proposals into a unified approach with consultations throughout 2025. The AI Act reaches full operational status by late summer 2026, while the UK pursues principles-based regulation, with the Financial Policy Committee assessing AI's financial stability implications in early 2025.

### Pensions Reform Uncertainty

The Government delayed the second stage of the Pensions Review, creating uncertainty surrounding pension reform pace and extent. Value-for-money assessments and the Pensions Dashboards Programme remain regulatory priorities. The FCA's targeted support regime proposals offer greater flexibility for pension decision-making, while Pensions Dashboard connection for larger schemes begins April 30, 2025.
