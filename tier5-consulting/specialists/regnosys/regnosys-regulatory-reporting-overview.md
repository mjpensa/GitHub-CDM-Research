# REGnosys Regulatory Reporting - Transparency as a Service

**Source:** REGnosys
**URL:** https://regnosys.com/solutions/regulatory-reporting/

---

## Overview

REGnosys provides a collaborative low-code platform for regulatory reporting, offering "a shared language to collect data in a transparent, consistent and truly digital way."

The platform enables firms and regulators to move data "from data buried in firms' source systems, to data shipped to regulators – all seamlessly traceable and auditable."

---

## Key Programs

### Digital Regulatory Reporting (DRR)

An industry-led initiative where participants use REGnosys to build "standardised, machine-executable expression of the reporting rules."

**Target Programs:**
- CFTC Rewrite (US, 2022)
- EMIR Refit (EU, 2024)
- Additional jurisdictions as they come into scope

## Customer Segments

### Financial Institutions

Focus on proving compliance through "end-to-end auditability of your reporting, down to single trade flow, available on-demand and on-going."

**Key Benefits:**
- Complete audit trail
- Trade-level traceability
- On-demand compliance verification

### Regulators

Emphasis on "machine readable and executable" regulations that enable "collecting high quality and comparable data across your regulated entities."

**Key Benefits:**
- Standardized data collection
- Cross-entity comparability

## Key Partnerships

### DTCC Collaboration

REGnosys partnered with DTCC to enable firms to validate DRR reports generated via Rosetta against DTCC repositories.

### G-20 TechSprint Recognition

ISDA and REGnosys won the G-20 TechSprint for Regulatory Reporting, developing a digital regulatory reporting pilot for the derivatives reporting regulations set by the Monetary Authority of Singapore (MAS) using the CDM.

## Rosetta Engine

The Rosetta engine is the first DRR-native trade data platform, allowing firms to:
- Author reporting logic directly against CDM definitions
- Execute validation rules
- Validate reports against regulatory requirements
