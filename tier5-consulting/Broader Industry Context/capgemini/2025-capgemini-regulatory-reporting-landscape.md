## Overview

Capgemini provides comprehensive analysis of the regulatory reporting landscape, including perspectives on CDM and DRR adoption, operational strategies, and upcoming regulatory changes.

## Current State Assessment

The current storm of regulatory change is starting to slow down, and global derivative reporting requirements are substantially harmonizing, presenting an opportunity for firms to:
- Evaluate their operating model and controls
- Optimize processes
- Implement strategic improvements

## Strategic Recommendations

Capgemini identifies three key strategic areas for firms:

### 1. Managed Services
The use of managed services and selective offshoring of business-as-usual processes can bring significant benefits:
- Exception management
- Reconciliations
- Operational oversight

### 2. Outsourcing Options
Strategic outsourcing of reporting architecture components:
- Eligibility checks
- Reconciliations
- Wholesale outsourcing to regulatory reporting platforms

### 3. Automation and Data Lineage
Critical focus areas:
- Investing in automation
- Establishing clear data lineage
- Improving data quality controls
