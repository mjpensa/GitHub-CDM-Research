## Overview

KPMG provides advisory services and thought leadership on derivatives regulatory reporting, including perspectives on CDM adoption, data quality challenges, and regulatory compliance in the post-trade space.

## 2024 Industry Assessment

According to Marcus Threadgold, Partner at KPMG Advisory (London) Limited:

> "It's been a big year for the industry with the plethora of Derivative re-writes going live and firms continuing to work through remediation activities."

### 2024 Regulatory Rewrites

KPMG tracked the following major regulatory changes:
- CFTC ISO 20022 and UPI introduction
- JFSA (Japan) Rewrite - April 1, 2024
- EU EMIR Refit - April 29, 2024
- UK EMIR Refit - September 30, 2024
- ASIC (Australia) Rewrites - October 21, 2024
- MAS (Singapore) Rewrites - October 21, 2024

## Data Quality Focus

KPMG observes that supervisory focus on data quality has been very clear in 2024, reinforced through:
- **ESMA Data Quality Reports**
- **FCA Market Watch publications**

### Ongoing Challenges

Despite general improvements in reported data across EMIR, SFTR and MiFID, challenges remain:
- Inconsistency across obligations
- Quality issues on Critical Data Elements (CDEs)

### Recommended Solutions

Firms continue to evolve and invest in governance and control environments to ensure data is complete, accurate and timely:

1. **Independent governance and control reviews**
2. **High-quality front-to-back data testing**
3. **Enhanced control environments**
4. **Automation investments**

## 2025 Regulatory Priorities

| June 2025 | EU | EMIR 3.0 active accounts at EU CCPs |
| July 25, 2025 | Canada | CSA OTC derivatives reporting amendments |
| September 29, 2025 | Hong Kong | HKMA reporting rule amendments |
| December 1, 2025 | UK | Bonds and derivatives transparency regime changes |

## CDM and DRR Context

KPMG recognizes the role of the Common Domain Model (CDM) and Digital Regulatory Reporting (DRR) in addressing industry challenges:

- **JSCC** announced production-parallel operations using DRR & CDM from June 2025
- **Global standardization** of regulatory reporting leveraging these frameworks
- **First CCP adoption** demonstrates viability for clearing houses
