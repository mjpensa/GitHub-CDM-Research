# JSCC: Global Standardization of Regulatory Reporting Leveraging DRR & CDM

**Source:** FINOS / Japan Securities Clearing Corporation (JSCC)
**Date:** January 15, 2025
**URL:** https://www.finos.org/press/from-finos-member-jscc-global-standardization-of-regulatory-reporting-leveraging-drr-cdm

---

## Full Press Release

The Japanese Securities Clearing Corporation (JSCC) announced it is the first CCP (Central Counterparty) and Japanese entity to adopt Digital Regulatory Reporting (DRR) and Common Domain Model (CDM) in a production environment.

### Key Announcement

JSCC plans to begin production-parallel operations for regulatory reporting to the Financial Services Agency (FSA) and the CFTC beginning in June 2025, using the standardized framework combining ISDA's DRR solution and FINOS's open source CDM.

### Implementation Timeline

| Date | Milestone |
|------|-----------|
| October 2023 | JSCC joined The Linux Foundation |
| June 2024 | JSCC joined FINOS |
| August 2024 | Proof of concept utilizing DRR/CDM completed |
| September 2024 | Received "Trailblazer Award" at Open Source in Finance Forum (New York) |
| June 2025 | Production-parallel operations commenced |

### Initial Implementation Scope

JSCC will conduct comparative verification for IRS (interest rate swaps) transaction reporting, then incrementally add CDS (credit default swaps) and expand DRR functionality.

### Key Quotes

**JSCC President Konuma Yasuyuki:**
> "I want JSCC to continue exploring initiatives with global standardization and open sourced ecosystems that contribute to advancement and stabilization of global financial markets."

**ISDA CEO Scott O'Malia:**
> "By converting industry-agreed reporting rules into code using the CDM, the ISDA DRR brings greater efficiency, automation, accuracy and consistency to derivatives trade reporting."

**FINOS Executive Director Gabriele Columbro:**
> "We are proud to witness JSCC's swift adoption of the CDM...as this momentum continues, we anticipate more firms investing in open tooling."

### Significance

- **First CCP globally** to adopt DRR and CDM in production
- **First Japanese financial institution** to implement DRR/CDM
- Demonstrates viability for clearing houses and CCPs
- Validates industry standardization efforts

## Additional Press Release: Launch Confirmation

**Source:** Japan Exchange Group
**Date:** July 2, 2025
**URL:** https://www.jpx.co.jp/english/corporate/news/news-releases/0060/20250702-01.html

JSCC commenced parallel production operations as planned on June 27, 2025. The corporation stated:

> "We are honored to be the first clearing organization globally and the first financial institution in Japan to announce the implementation of DRR and CDM in a production environment."

### Future Plans

- Add CDS (credit default swaps) to DRR/CDM based regulatory reporting
- Incrementally expand DRR functionality together with ISDA's plans
- Increase the number of target regulatory authorities
- Conduct comparative analysis with current system using live data
- Continue medium- to long-term pilot projects
