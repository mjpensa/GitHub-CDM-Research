# DTCC Collaborates with REGnosys on Regulatory Reporting

**Source:** Markets Media
**Published:** April 11, 2024
**Author:** Terry Flanagan
**URL:** https://www.marketsmedia.com/dtcc-collaborates-with-regnosys-on-regulatory-reporting/

---

## Full Article Content

DTCC announced a collaboration with REGnosys to support industry adoption of the ISDA Digital Regulatory Reporting (DRR) solution and assist with compliance regarding global derivatives trade reporting requirements.

### Partnership Focus

REGnosys's Rosetta platform, described as "the first ISDA DRR-native regulatory reporting platform," is now connecting to DTCC's Global Trade Repository (GTR) testing environment. This enables firms to test trade reporting submissions using the ISDA DRR and verify acceptance by DTCC's GTR system.

### Technical Framework

The ISDA DRR leverages the Common Domain Model (CDM)—an open-source data standard for financial products and trades—to convert market practices into machine-executable code. The system transforms trade data into ISO 20022 format as mandated by regulators.

### Regulatory Coverage

**Initial Phase:**
- Japan's Financial Services Agency (JFSA)
- EU's European Market Infrastructure Regulation (EMIR)

**Subsequent Phases:**
- UK EMIR
- Australian Securities and Investments Commission (ASIC)
- Singapore's Monetary Authority (MAS)

1. **Validation Testing** - Firms can test DRR-generated reports against DTCC GTR
2. **ISO 20022 Compliance** - Automatic transformation to required format
3. **Multi-Jurisdiction Support** - Single framework for multiple regulatory regimes
4. **Streamlined Implementation** - Reduced complexity for market participants
