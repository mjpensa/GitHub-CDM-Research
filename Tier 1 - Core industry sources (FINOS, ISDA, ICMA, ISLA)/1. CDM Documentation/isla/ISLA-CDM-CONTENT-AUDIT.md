## Publicly Available ISLA CDM Content

### 1. ISLA CDM Overview Page
**URL:** https://www.islaemea.org/common-domain-model/
**Status:** Public

The CDM is a data model that provides a standard format for financial products and transactions in the capital markets industry. It is intended to improve data quality, increase efficiency, and reduce costs by creating a common language that enables automated trade processing and reduces the need for costly customizations.

ISLA has been working with securities financing subject matter experts to model components in the CDM for the Securities Lending market.

### 2. Securities Lending MVP Merged into CDM
**Source:** ISLA Press Release
**Status:** Public

Key announcement that securities lending minimum viable product has been merged into the main CDM codebase, representing a significant milestone for standardization.

### 3. ISLA Document Digitisation Working Group Launch
**Source:** ISLA News
**URL:** https://www.islaemea.org/news/isla-launches-document-digitisation-working-group/
**Status:** Public

ISLA launched a working group dedicated to digitising the Global Master Securities Lending Agreement (GMSLA). The ISLA Document Digitisation Working Group focuses on encoding the business outcomes from the ISLA Clause Library & Taxonomy into the Common Domain Model (CDM), integrating the two foundational pillars of ISLA's digital standardisation efforts.

## ISLA CDM Integration Details (from Public Sources)

### GMSLA in CDM

A full-CDM will integrate all aspects of a transaction including:
- Product representation
- Event representation and outcome
- Legal documentation impacting a transaction, such as a GMSLA or a collateral agreement
- Process sequencing and outcome
- Reference data translation

The CDM now includes the GMSLA codified in the same language as the product definition, meaning counterparties now have a complete representation of their transactions.

### Industry Collaboration

ISLA has been working with ISDA and the International Capital Markets Association (ICMA) to apply a CDM to securities lending transactions. ICMA has been doing similarly for repo and bond trades.

### Key Priorities (from Chris Rayner, Senior Associate, Market Infrastructure and Technology at ISLA)

> "There has been considerable demand from members to expand the Association's work on pre-trade processing and digitisation of legal documentation. In standardising the initial negotiation of the trade we have been able to reduce the probability of breaks in the post-trade lifecycle."

## Future Outlook (from Public Sources)

Looking further ahead, there is scope for ISLA to work together with ISDA and ICMA on new regulatory requirements, including:
- SEC 10c-1a reporting requirements
- SFTR amendments

As the market moves to T+1 settlement, the industry is reaching an inflection point where legacy infrastructure and processes will not provide the required efficiency.

> "Standardization makes chaotic human transactions intelligible to and manageable by computers by providing a common foundation for technologies like DLT, smart contracts, cloud computing, and AI. ISLA believes the CDM would be the necessary bridge linking the securities lending industry with a digitized and automated future."
