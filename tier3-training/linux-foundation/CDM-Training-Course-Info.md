## Target Audience

- Financial professionals in trading, operations, risk management and compliance
- Anyone interested in standardization and automation of financial data and processes

## Prerequisites

- No prior knowledge of CDM required
- Basic understanding of financial instruments and operational processes beneficial

## Learning Outcomes

Earners of the LFEL1016 badge:
- Understand CDM's principles and coverage
- Know the different financial products and business processes supported by CDM
- Can explain the various components of CDM and how to use them in implementations
- Know how to set up CDM and contribute to the project

## About TradeHeader (Course Developer)

TradeHeader is:
- Co-creator of the Common Domain Model (CDM) framework
- One of the few maintainers of the standard
- Participates in day-to-day development
- Partner to FINOS on CDM
- Provides advisory and implementation services on CDM
