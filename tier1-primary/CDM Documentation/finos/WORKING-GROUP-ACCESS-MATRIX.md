## Meeting Participation

### How to Join Working Groups

**FINOS Working Groups:**
- Open participation model
- Join via FINOS community calendar
- No membership required

**Steps:**
1. Visit https://www.finos.org/community-calendar
2. Find relevant working group meeting
3. Register for calendar invite
4. Participate in meetings

**Note:** While meeting participation is open, not all meeting materials may be publicly archived.

## Recommendations for Access

### For Maximum Public Access

1. **GitHub** - Primary source for technical content
2. **CDM Portal** - Best for documentation
3. **FINOS Website** - News and announcements
4. **Working Group Participation** - Open to all

### For Full Access

| Content Need | Requirement |
|--------------|-------------|
| ISLA CDM content | ISLA membership |
| ISDA DRR participation | ISDA membership + active contribution |
| ICMA full content | ICMA membership |
